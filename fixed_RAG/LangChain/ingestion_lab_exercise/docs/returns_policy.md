# Returns and Refunds

Customers may return unused products within 30 days of delivery. The item must include its original packaging and proof of purchase.

## Damaged items

Report damaged items within 48 hours of delivery. Customer Care may request photographs before arranging a replacement or refund.

## Refund timing

Approved card refunds normally appear within five to seven working days. Bank processing times can vary.

## Exceptions

Personalised products cannot normally be returned unless they arrive damaged or do not match the confirmed order.

## Contact

Include the order number, delivery date, and a short description when contacting Customer Care.
